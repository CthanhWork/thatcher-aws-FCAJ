## Sharing

This workshop is designed to be reproducible: another learner can follow the prerequisites, deploy the core API, validate the principal flows, and understand which parts are production extensions versus the working MVP.

## Feedback Focus

Useful review questions for this workshop include:

- Is the architecture explanation clear about what is deployed today?
- Are the service choices appropriate for the product stage and expected traffic?
- Are the security boundaries and cleanup warnings specific enough?
- Which production enhancement should be prioritized first: CI/CD, WAF, asynchronous jobs, or high availability?

Feedback can be incorporated into the next workshop iteration to improve reliability, cost visibility, and the developer onboarding experience.
