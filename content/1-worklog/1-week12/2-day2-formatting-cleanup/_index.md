## Why I Did This

After weeks of writing, a few pages needed a consistency pass so the report feels like one coherent document.

## Implementation Steps

### Step 1: Normalize Headings

I checked the heading structure so the weekly pages read the same way.

### Step 2: Reduce Duplicate Detail

Anything too specific for the week overview was moved into the relevant day page.

### Step 3: Keep the Tone Consistent

I aligned the language and layout so the worklog feels uniform across all weeks.

## Verification

- Confirm overview pages stay concise.
- Confirm detailed content lives in day pages.
- Confirm headings follow the same pattern.

## What I Learned

- A clean information hierarchy makes long documentation easier to read.
- Less duplication makes updates easier later.
- Consistent formatting improves the overall quality of the report.

## Application to Travel Platform

This pass makes the report easier to review and much easier to extend or maintain.
